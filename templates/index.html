This report traces that change, then examines the surrounding evidence on organizational scale, training cost and compute, talent, evaluation attention, open-model supply, and business adoption. Across five chapters, we connect these views while keeping each dataset’s limits and the distinction between association and causation clear.

CHAPTER 01 · OBSERVATION

The Flip: Who holds the frontier positions?

Begin with the observed shift. In the last available Arena overall snapshot of February 2024, 40 of the Top 50 models had open weights. By September 2026, only nine did. Every model in the final Top 10 was closed-weight. Open weights do not necessarily mean fully open-source AI.

KEY FINDINGOpen-weight share of the Top 50: 80% → 18%February 2024 to September 2026; both snapshots contain 50 models. The path was not monotonic.

CHAPTER 03 · THRESHOLD

The Cost Cliff: How expensive is frontier competition?

A single GPT-4.5 training run is estimated at $366M. Against that benchmark, the dashboard’s illustrative academic budgets cover just 0.005–0.04 of a run, and some startup funding falls short. Academia’s share of notable models fell from 15.1% to 1.1% between 2023 and 2025.

KEY FINDINGPriced out of frontier pre-training, academia’s share of notable models collapsed15.1% in 2023 → 1.1% in 2025. The charts do not establish that cost caused the decline.

CHAPTER 04 · PARALLEL TRENDS

Parallel Trends: Can attention and supply grow together?

Chapter 4 looks beyond the leaderboard. From 2024 to 2025, proprietary models gained a larger share of recorded Arena evaluations while new Hugging Face repositories also grew. The dashboard adds shifting model exposure, slowing but positive U.S. talent netflow, and rising business AI adoption. These parallel measures provide ecosystem context, not evidence of user migration or causation.

KEY FINDINGProprietary evaluation share and HF additions rose togetherAcross 2024Q1–2025Q4, the share moved from 50.28% to 56.17%, while quarterly new repositories moved from 118,252 to 332,098. Both series fluctuated.

READING NOTES

Methods & limits

These figures summarize data supplied with the team dashboards. Open each chapter to inspect the original interactions, filters, and more detailed annotations.

01Compare metrics on their own termsMarket value, revenue run rate, evaluation share, and repository additions have different definitions and denominators.
02Keep missing values missingUnobserved months, organizations, and metrics remain blank. Several series have incomplete coverage in 2026.
03Parallel trends are not causationCost growth, ranking changes, and business adoption can coincide. The available data do not establish movement of resources or users.
